Patient random effects

Random intercept

Every patient gets their own starting level u₀ᵢ, but all share the same slope. The fitted lines are parallel: patients differ in how high their blood pressure is, not in how fast it falls.

Adding a random slope

Each patient also gets a slope deviation u₁ᵢ, so lines can fan out or cross. The model estimates the spread of slopes (τ₁) and whether high starters tend to drop faster (the correlation).

Shrinkage

Turn on Separate per-patient fits. Those dashed lines use only one patient's visits. The mixed-model lines sit closer to the mean, most of all for patients with few or noisy visits, because they borrow strength from the whole group.
